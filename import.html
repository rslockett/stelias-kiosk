Step 1

Paste the weekly email

The best way is to save the email as a file and drop it here — that keeps the headings and links intact, so the split comes out much cleaner. In Gmail, open the newsletter, click the ⋮ menu at the top right of the message, and choose Download message. You'll get a .eml file.

Or paste the text instead

Copying and pasting works too, but Gmail throws away the formatting that marks headings, so you'll have more tidying up to do.

Step 2

Check each announcement

A weekly newsletter holds far more than belongs on a coffee hour screen. Untick anything that isn't worth a slide, edit what came out wrong, and watch the length bar — an announcement that runs over gets trimmed on the TV, so it's better to shorten it here.

Nothing yet. Paste an email above and press “Split into announcements”.
Step 3

Put them in the Sheet

Open the Sheet ↗

Copy rows by hand instead

Then:

  1. Open the announcements Google Sheet.
  2. Select every old row under the headers and delete them.
  3. Click cell A2 and paste.

The TV picks up the change on its own within a few minutes. Nobody needs to touch the Raspberry Pi.
